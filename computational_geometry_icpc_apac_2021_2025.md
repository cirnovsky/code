# Computational Geometry Problems in ICPC Asia-Pacific Regionals, 2021–2025

This list treats “past five years” as the five completed contest years **2021–2025**, and “Asia Pacific” according to the [ICPC Archive](https://icpcarchive.github.io/) and [QOJ Asia-Pacific category](https://qoj.ac/category/23): Indonesia, Japan, Korea, the Philippines, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam.

The Asia-Pacific Championship itself and contests belonging to the Asia East or Asia West superregions are excluded.

## Clear computational-geometry problems

### 2021

- Jakarta I — **Stable Planetary System** — closest pair of points; sorting by radius. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Indonesia/2021%20Asia%20Jakarta%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Yokohama J — **The Cross Covers Everything** — point ordering and covering a point set with an axis-aligned cross. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Japan/2021%20Asia%20Yokohama%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Seoul E — **Grid Triangle** — counting triangles in a 3D integer lattice.
- Seoul I — **Security System** — nonintersecting orthogonal paths inside a rectilinear polygon. [Seoul problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/South%20Korea/2021%20Asia%20Seoul%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Taipei F — **What a Colorful Wall** — visibility and painting of overlapping axis-aligned rectangles. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Taiwan/2021%20Asia%20Taipei%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Hanoi H — **Hoang & Vuong** — segment game whose winning condition is creating a convex polygon. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Vietnam/2021%20Asia%20Hanoi%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)

### 2022

- Jakarta B — **Magical Barrier** — arrangements and intersections of all segments between planar points. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Indonesia/2022%20Asia%20Jakarta%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Yokohama J — **Traveling Salesperson in an Island** — shortest closed route inside a simple polygon; polygon-boundary and geodesic reasoning. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Japan/2022%20Asia%20Yokohama%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Seoul B — **Castle Design** — construct or minimize the perimeter of a monotone rectilinear polygon from its turn sequence.
- Seoul C — **Empty Quadrilaterals** — count quadrilaterals containing no other input point. [Seoul problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/South%20Korea/2022%20Asia%20Seoul%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Taoyuan H — **Meeting Places** — minimum enclosing circle combined with dynamic programming. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Taiwan/2022%20Asia%20Taoyuan%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Manila J — **Junior Steiner Three** — rectilinear or Manhattan Steiner tree for three points. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Philippines/2022%20Asia%20Manila%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- HCMC E — **Emotional Damage** — convex-polygon area, centroid, and landing-side probabilities. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Vietnam/2022%20Asia%20Ho%20Chi%20Minh%20City%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)

### 2023

- Jakarta D — **Space Exploration** — tangents and segment intersection with a convex polygon; shortest Euclidean polyline. [Jakarta archive](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Indonesia.html)
- Yokohama K — **Probing the Disk** *(interactive)* — reconstruct a hidden disk using chord-length probes. [Problem set](https://icpcarchive.github.io/Asia%20Pacific%20Contests/Japan/2023%20Asia%20Yokohama%20Regional%20Contest/problems.pdf)
- Seoul C — **Farm** — partition a rectilinear polygon containing points into rectangles. [Official problem set](https://static.icpckorea.net/2023/regional/problemset.pdf)
- Hue A — **Area Query** — dynamically insert noncrossing diagonals into a convex polygon and answer component-area queries.
- Hue E — **Eclipse** — rotate one convex polygon around the origin so that it fits inside another. [Vietnam archive](https://icpcvn.github.io/)

### 2024

- Yokohama F — **The Farthest Point** — farthest surface-geodesic point on a rectangular cuboid, using unfoldings. [Official problem set](https://icpc.iisf.or.jp/2024-yokohama/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/2024/11/problems.pdf)
- Seoul C — **Colorful Quadrants** — find an axis-aligned quadrant division containing four distinct colors; orthogonal range searching. [QOJ](https://qoj.ac/problem/10537)
- Seoul I — **Square Stamping** — cover points on three horizontal lines with a minimum number of fixed-size squares. [QOJ](https://qoj.ac/problem/10543)
- Seoul L — **Triangle** — choose lattice points on triangle sides to minimize or maximize area. [QOJ](https://qoj.ac/problem/10546)
- Taichung L — **Building Castle** — minimum symmetric-difference area between a convex polygon and a point-symmetric convex polygon. [Codeforces contest](https://codeforces.com/contest/2041)
- Hanoi A — **Approaching Hurricane** — area affected by a moving or growing circular region intersecting a simple polygon. [Official problem set](https://icpcvn.github.io/2024/regional/problems.pdf)

### 2025

- Yokohama F — **Astral Geometry** *(interactive)* — reconstruct distances among unknown 3D lattice points.
- Yokohama L — **Common Tangent Lines** — minimally translate four lines so they become common tangents of two disks. [Official problem set](https://icpc.jp/2025/docs/all_contest_en_with_cover.pdf)
- Seoul C — **Bay** — planar lattice-area calculation using cross products and Pick’s theorem.
- Seoul K — **Quadrants** — rotate two perpendicular lines while maintaining point counts in four quadrants. [Official Seoul problem set](https://static.icpckorea.net/2025/regional/problemset.pdf), [QOJ](https://qoj.ac/problem/15471)
- Taichung C — **Circles Are Far from Each Other** — arrange nested or disjoint circles with collinear centers.
- Taichung I — **Birthday** — cut a circular cake with a line, keep all marked points on one side, and maximize the smaller area. [Codeforces contest](https://codeforces.com/contest/2172), [QOJ](https://qoj.ac/problem/15401)
- Bangkok C — **The Drift King** — minimum Euclidean distance from a point to an infinite periodic path of quarter-circle arcs.
- Bangkok L — **Laser** — weighted Manhattan nearest-receiver queries with one orthogonal turn. [Official problem set](https://codeforces.com/gym/106164/attachments/download/34173/icpc-bkk-2025.pdf)
- Manila C — **The Drift King** — the same geometry problem as Bangkok C. [Official problem set](https://codeforces.com/gym/106262/attachments/download/34830/main.pdf)
- HCMC C — **Cake Cutting** — dynamic removal and area queries for axis-aligned quadrants of a rectangle.
- HCMC H — **Hamiltonian Path Remix** — shortest Euclidean Hamiltonian path through the vertices of a convex polygon. [Official problem set](https://icpcvn.github.io/2025/regional/problems.pdf)

There are **35 regional appearances** representing **34 distinct clear problems**. **The Drift King** appeared at both the Bangkok and Manila regionals.

## Geometry-adjacent problems

These have geometric input or interpretation, but their intended solution is primarily combinatorics, graph algorithms, number theory, or general data structures.

- 2021 Hanoi C — **Coloring Polygon** — noncrossing diagonals of a convex polygon.
- 2022 Taoyuan I — **Cell Nuclei Detection** — rectangle intersection-over-union plus matching.
- 2022 HCMC F — **Fabulous Activity** — expected covered length of collinear segments.
- 2023 Yokohama C — **Ferris Wheel** — noncrossing matching of circularly ordered points, mainly Burnside's lemma and combinatorics.
- 2023 Jakarta M — **Triangle Construction** — noncrossing combinatorial construction on polygon sides.
- 2023 Taoyuan E — **Slabstones Rearrangement** — horizontally translate rectangles; overlap constraints reduce to a DAG and longest path.
- 2023 Hue C — **Crop Circle Conundrum** — patterns on a hexagonal grid.
- 2025 Bangkok G — **Galactic Adventure Agency** — 3D Manhattan-distance transforms on a tree.
- 2025 Bangkok K — **Kickshot Tournament** — 45-degree billiards in a rectangle, ultimately solved with gcd or CRT reasoning.

Depending on how narrowly computational geometry is defined, 2025 HCMC C, **Cake Cutting**, can also be placed in this section because its main machinery is orthogonal range and area maintenance rather than continuous geometry.

## Coverage notes

- No clear computational-geometry problem appeared in the **2024 or 2025 Jakarta** sets.
- Singapore did not host an ICPC regional during this interval.
- Grid problems with no significant geometric reasoning were excluded.
- Problems that merely use coordinates as labels or weights were excluded unless a geometric metric or geometric structure is central to the solution.
